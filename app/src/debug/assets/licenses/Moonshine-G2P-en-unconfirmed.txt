Moonshine English pronunciation (G2P) data

Files: en_us/dict_filtered_heteronyms.tsv, en_us/g2p-config.json,
en_us/oov/model.ort and en_us/oov/onnx-config.json, downloaded from
https://download.moonshine.ai/tts/ when you download a voice.

License: not yet confirmed.

Moonshine's LICENSE puts its code and speech-to-text models under the MIT
License, but excludes its text-to-speech and pronunciation data from that
default without naming the license those files are under. The dictionary
appears to be derived from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (BSD-2-Clause).

Distillery has asked Moonshine AI to confirm the license of these files.
Until it is confirmed to be an open-source license, text to speech is only
included in dev builds of Distillery, never in releases. If it turns out not
to be open source, text to speech will be removed.
