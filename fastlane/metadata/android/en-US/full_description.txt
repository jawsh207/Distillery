Distillery is a speech-to-text and text-to-speech engine for Android. It plugs into Android's standard speech recognition service, so any app that offers voice input can use it: keyboards and apps get live dictation with no popup and no app switch, and apps can hand it audio recordings to transcribe. It is also a system text-to-speech engine, so apps that read text aloud can use its natural-sounding voices.

<b>Private by design</b>
Speech is recognized entirely on your phone with Moonshine, an open-source speech model built for live speech, and spoken with Kokoro, an open-source voice model. Distillery never sends audio or text anywhere and needs no account. The only network use is the one-time download of the speech model and voices you choose.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Works as the phone's default voice input (Android's RecognitionService)</li>
<li>Live partial results while you speak, then the final text</li>
<li>Stops on its own when you pause</li>
<li>Transcribes recordings other apps hand over: AMR, M4A/3GP, MP3, Ogg, FLAC, WAV</li>
<li>Choose Tiny, Small or Medium to trade speed for accuracy</li>
<li>Material 3 Expressive design that follows your wallpaper colors</li>
<li>Text to speech with 28 American and British English voices</li>
<li>Reads numbers, dates, money and abbreviations the way people say them</li>
<li>Add your own pronunciations for names and places</li>
<li>Built-in test bench for dictation, recordings and speech</li>
</ul>

<b>Open source</b>
Distillery is licensed under the GPL-3.0. Moonshine and the English streaming models are MIT-licensed; Kokoro and its voices are Apache-2.0. Distillery never downloads models under non-open-source licenses.

Requires Android 16. English only for now.
